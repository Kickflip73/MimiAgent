# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

MimiAgent contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, professional, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or technical preference.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- discussing ideas and code without attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- acknowledging tradeoffs and correcting mistakes openly;
- respecting privacy and consent;
- helping new contributors understand project conventions;
- focusing disagreements on evidence and project goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate exposure of private information, or retaliating against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project chat, events, and private communication related to project participation. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this policy. Depending on severity and history, they may issue a warning, require a pause in participation, or impose a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the same channel described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical; retaliation is not tolerated.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from handling the report.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
